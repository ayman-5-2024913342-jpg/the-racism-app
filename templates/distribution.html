How this works

Only people who opt in contribute, and only after saving a result. Each contribution adds a single lightness number (L*). Nothing else is included, and individual records are never shown.

Honest limits

  • Real data appears only once at least {{ min_n }} contributions exist. Until then you see clearly labeled synthetic data.
  • Bars with fewer than 5 contributions are hidden to protect privacy.
  • Lighting, exposure, filters and who chose to participate shape this chart. It is not a population statistic, and no percentiles or bell curves are drawn.
  • You can withdraw contributions any time in Settings.
