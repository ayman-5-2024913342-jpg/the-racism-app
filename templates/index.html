HOW THE ESTIMATE WORKS

Measured, then explained.

A transparent set of rules, not a black box. They are heuristics, not a validated clinical method.

  1. Check the file. The image is validated by its actual content, decoded in memory, and stripped of metadata.
  2. Find skin pixels. Skin-colored pixels are selected inside your crop; background, deep shadows and clipped highlights are excluded.
  3. Measure color. The median color is taken in CIELAB. A shade band comes from the Individual Typology Angle, and the undertone from the hue angle.
  4. Score the evidence. A reliability score reflects coverage, sample size, consistency, exposure, detail and lighting color. Low evidence means an "inconclusive" undertone.

Your photo belongs to you.

Photos are analyzed in memory and are never written to disk by this app. HTTPS protects a photo in transit, but it does not make a service private by itself, so here is exactly what happens.

Get started ↗

Processed, not kept

  • The uploaded photo and your crop exist in server memory only while they are analyzed.
  • Photos are not stored in the database or logged. Logs never contain images or colors.
  • Your host or proxy may see standard request metadata such as IP address and timing.

Kept only if you save

  • Computed values: sampled color, shade band, undertone, reliability and a palette, plus the name you enter. Saved results are public: anyone can view them.
  • A random browser identifier (hashed on the server) so only your browser can delete your results.
  • Unsaved results are deleted automatically after {{ ttl_hours }} hours. You can delete anything, any time, from Settings.
  • Anonymous contribution to the distribution is off by default and shares only a lightness number.
