How this estimate was made, and its limits

{{ disclaimer }}

  • Skin pixels are chosen with a YCrCb + HSV range test, then outliers are trimmed and the median CIELAB color is used (algorithm v{{ r.algorithm_version }}).
  • Undertone comes from the hue angle of that color. Colored light, filters and white balance shift it more than they shift lightness.
  • CIELAB is only approximately perceptually uniform, and assumes standard viewing conditions that a phone photo does not provide.
  • The rules are documented heuristics. They have not been scientifically validated for uncalibrated photos.
